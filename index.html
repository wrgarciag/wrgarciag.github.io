William García

Ph.D. Candidate, Global Health Metrics and Implementation Science
Department of Global Health, University of Washington

I am a health economist. I study how investments in health can prevent disease, extend lives, and improve economic well-being.

My work brings together health economics, simulation modeling, and policy evaluation to inform decisions about noncommunicable disease prevention in the United States and globally.

I am a Research Assistant on the Disease Control Priorities Project, 4th Edition (DCP4), an External Research Associate at PROESA, Universidad Icesi, and a Fulbright Scholar. I am also pursuing a concentration in Social Statistics at the Center for Statistics and the Social Sciences (CSSS).

I am on the academic job market.

Fields: Health economics · Health metrics · Decision science

Research interests: Economic evaluation and benefit–cost analysis · Simulation modeling · Noncommunicable disease prevention · Cardiovascular health · Valuation of health and longevity · Health policy in the U.S. and LMICs
